## Position and boundary

The site should help owners, designers, facility teams, and buyers define, commission, operate, and renovate a built swimming pool. It owns project briefing, site inputs, functional planning, shell/waterproofing interfaces, hydraulics, treatment, finishes, safety, electrical coordination, construction QA, operation, troubleshooting, and procurement.

It does not provide structural, geotechnical, hydraulic, electrical, public-health, accessibility, or code sign-off; chemical dosing instructions without measured water and product evidence; DIY structural repair; or location-swapped contractor pages. Project dimensions and system values require current standards and competent project-specific design.

## Evidence gates

- Pool geometry, depth, shell thickness/reinforcement, joints, waterproof build-up, pipe diameter, turnover/flow, pump/filter capacity, suction arrangement, electrical protection, barriers, and accessibility require current standards and competent project-specific design.
- Water-treatment claims require measured parameters, intended use, bather load, source water, treatment system, chemical product data, and current public-health guidance where applicable.
- Never recommend chemical mixing or blind dosing. Chemical handling requires product safety data, trained personnel, ventilation/storage controls, and measurement before/after.
- Leak, crack, settlement, electrical, suction, or structural symptoms require isolation and qualified assessment; do not publish DIY structural, underwater electrical, or circulation-safety modifications.
- Slip, chemical, UV, fire, load, energy, acoustic, corrosion, service-life, warranty, and performance claims require named tests/products and documented project conditions.
- Prices, quantities, schedules, productivity, and savings require dated like-for-like scopes.
- Project photos, test results, water logs, incident accounts, and case studies require provenance and permission.
- Hotels, apartments, athletic facilities, and waterparks require owner procedures, supervision, emergency planning, capacity assessment, and current local regulatory review.
